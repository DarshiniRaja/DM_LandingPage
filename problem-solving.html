Google Ads "Getting plenty of clicks, but spending too much without qualified leads"
▼

The Root Cause Diagnosis:

Your campaigns are running on broad match with smart bidding without strict negative keyword fencing. Google's algorithm is bidding on informational keywords like "how to do free x" or "jobs in x", burning 45% of your ad spend on non-buyers.

The ScalePulse Solution:

1. We deploy a 500+ negative keyword fortress immediately.
2. We restructure to high-intent transactional search queries & targeted Performance Max asset groups.
3. We implement offline conversion tracking to feed Google only closed-won revenue signals.

Meta Ads "Meta ads work for 4 days, then performance crashes and CPMs spike"
▼

The Root Cause Diagnosis:

You are relying on only 1 or 2 static creatives. Meta's auction fatigues small audiences in days. Additionally, iOS privacy updates stripped 30% of your pixel tracking, leaving Meta unable to find real buyers.

The ScalePulse Solution:

1. We institute a High-Velocity Creative Sandbox delivering 15-20 tested hooks/angles per week.
2. We install First-Party Conversions API (CAPI) on your server with 95%+ event match quality.
3. We scale winning variations horizontally across Advantage+ audience clusters.

SEO & Organic "We get organic search visitors, but they never inquire or buy anything"
▼

The Root Cause Diagnosis:

Your agency ranked you for top-of-funnel informational blog posts that attract college students and researchers, not decision-makers with credit cards in hand. There are zero clear conversion paths or comparison tables on those pages.

The ScalePulse Solution:

1. We pivot organic strategy exclusively to Bottom-of-Funnel (BOFU) commercial intent keywords ("Best X for Y", "Alternative to Z").
2. We embed interactive lead widgets and audit pop-ups directly inside high-traffic ranking articles.
3. We build digital PR links directly to revenue-generating service pages.

Funnel & CRM "People submit inquiries but ghost when we call or email them back"
▼

The Root Cause Diagnosis:

Speed-to-lead latency. Studies prove that contacting a lead after 15 minutes drops conversion likelihood by 400%. If your sales rep takes 24 hours to email them, they have already signed with your competitor.

The ScalePulse Solution:

1. Automated 90-second SMS & email confirmation triggered the instant they submit.
2. Direct Google Meet / Calendly embedded on the Thank You page so they book their own slot while intent is at 100%.
3. Pre-call indoctrination sequence that warms them up with customer case studies before the call.
